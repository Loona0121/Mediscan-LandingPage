FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything you need to know about scanning, inventory, and reports.

How does expiry scanning work?
Point your camera at the front of the product first, then flip it to the back/expiry label. MediScan is an AI-powered system that extracts the product name and expiry date automatically.
Why is my scan result inaccurate?
Blurry photos, glare, or partially covered text can affect OCR accuracy. Try rescanning in good lighting and holding the camera steady.
Can I edit the expiry date after scanning?
Yes. On the Verify Scan Results screen, you can manually correct the product name, expiry date, and category before saving.
How do I add or remove staff accounts?
Admin accounts can manage staff under Settings → Staff Management. Only admins can add or remove staff members.
How do I generate an inventory report?
From the Inventory screen, tap the report icon to open Generate Report. Choose a report type, export format (PDF or CSV), and reporting period.
